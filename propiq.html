Built by an investor, for investors

Propiq wasn't designed in a boardroom. It was built by a property investor who was tired of juggling spreadsheets — so every feature solves a problem a real investor actually has: knowing your true cashflow, seeing your equity, clearing debt faster, and walking into tax time already organised.

What's included

Everything your portfolio needs.

From a single investment property to a large portfolio — Propiq gives you a complete, accurate picture at all times.

Portfolio dashboard

See your entire portfolio at a glance — value, equity, LVR and net cashflow — plus growth since purchase and smart insights that surface your top performer, fixed rates about to expire, and the usable equity available for your next purchase.

Income & expense tracking

Log rental income and all expenses with custom categories — body corporate, insurance, repairs, management fees, and more. Organised by financial year.

Tax reports

Generate end-of-financial-year tax reports per property. Export as PDF, email directly (with your advisor cc'd), or copy as CSV for your accountant. Includes rental income, full expense categorisation, and depreciation deductions.

Debt elimination plan Investor+

Add every debt — home loan, credit cards, car, personal — set how much of your income to commit, and see exactly when you'll be debt-free. The snowball rolls each cleared repayment onto the next, with your payoff date and total interest saved.

Depreciation Investor+

Build a Division 40 and Division 43 item list for each property, email it straight to your quantity surveyor, then capture the yearly figures — they flow into your tax report as a deduction automatically.

Tenancy management

Track tenants, weekly rent, lease expiry dates, and vacancy rates. Get visibility on leases expiring within 90 days so you're never caught off guard.

Maintenance log

Keep a full record of maintenance work across your portfolio — date, description, amount, and category. Invaluable at tax time and for tracking recurring issues.

LVR & equity tracking

Automatically calculates your Loan-to-Value Ratio and equity position for each property. Stay on top of your borrowing capacity across the whole portfolio.

Ownership structure

Record co-ownership for each property with percentage splits. Useful for joint investments, SMSF holdings, or trust structures where ownership isn't 100% individual.

Advisor access

Invite a property advisor or accountant to view your portfolio. They get read-only access to your data — no shared logins, full privacy.

Live chat with Advisor

Chat directly with your connected advisor inside the app. Ask questions about your portfolio, get feedback on decisions, and stay aligned — all in one place, powered by PropIQ.

Bills & Obligations

Track every recurring bill across your portfolio — water, council rates, land tax, insurance, strata. Auto-generates payment schedules, flags overdue and upcoming payments, and sends personalised reminders so nothing slips through the cracks.

Land tax calculator

Estimate your annual land tax liability for any Australian state or territory. Enter a land value for single-property planning, or load your entire portfolio to see state-by-state aggregated land tax exposure at a glance.

How it works

Up and running in minutes.

No setup fees, no training required. Your first property is free.

STEP 01

Create your free account

Sign up in under a minute. No credit card needed — your first property is free to track, with no time limit.

STEP 02

Add your property

Enter your property details — address, purchase price, current value, mortgage, rental income, and ownership structure. Takes about 5 minutes per property.

STEP 03

Log income & expenses

Record rent received, repairs, management fees, insurance, and any other income or expense. Organised by financial year and ready for tax time.

STEP 04

Export your tax report

At the end of the financial year, generate a full tax report. Export it as a PDF, email it, or copy it as CSV to hand straight to your accountant.
