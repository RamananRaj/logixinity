What's included

Everything your portfolio needs.

From a single investment property to a large portfolio — Propertiq gives you a complete, accurate picture at all times.

Portfolio dashboard

See your entire portfolio at a glance — current value, loan balances, equity, LVR, annual rental, and net cashflow across all properties in one table.

Income & expense tracking

Log rental income and all expenses with custom categories — body corporate, insurance, repairs, management fees, and more. Organised by financial year.

Tax reports

Generate end-of-financial-year tax reports per property. Export as PDF, email directly, or copy as CSV for your accountant. Includes rental income breakdown and full expense categorisation.

Tenancy management

Track tenants, weekly rent, lease expiry dates, and vacancy rates. Get visibility on leases expiring within 90 days so you're never caught off guard.

Maintenance log

Keep a full record of maintenance work across your portfolio — date, description, amount, and category. Invaluable at tax time and for tracking recurring issues.

LVR & equity tracking

Automatically calculates your Loan-to-Value Ratio and equity position for each property. Stay on top of your borrowing capacity across the whole portfolio.

Ownership structure

Record co-ownership for each property with percentage splits. Useful for joint investments, SMSF holdings, or trust structures where ownership isn't 100% individual.

Advisor access

Invite a property advisor or accountant to view your portfolio. They get read access to help you make better decisions without needing to share logins.

How it works

Up and running in minutes.

No setup fees, no training required. Your first property is free.

STEP 01

Create your free account

Sign up in under a minute. No credit card needed — your first property is free to track, with no time limit.

STEP 02

Add your property

Enter your property details — address, purchase price, current value, mortgage, rental income, and ownership structure. Takes about 5 minutes per property.

STEP 03

Log income & expenses

Record rent received, repairs, management fees, insurance, and any other income or expense. Organised by financial year and ready for tax time.

STEP 04

Export your tax report

At the end of the financial year, generate a full tax report. Export it as a PDF, email it, or copy it as CSV to hand straight to your accountant.
