How it works

Connected to Xero
in minutes.

No lengthy onboarding. No data migration. Connect, review, and let the AI get to work.

STEP 01

Create your account

Sign up for your 30-day free trial. No credit card required — just your email and you're in.

STEP 02

Connect Xero

Authorise Gen LXI to read your Xero data. Your chart of accounts, transactions, and bank feeds sync automatically — nothing to export or import.

STEP 03

AI starts working

The AI agent analyses your Xero data, identifies key trends, flags anomalies, and starts generating business insights. You get a clear dashboard of what matters — nothing more.

STEP 04

Review & approve

You stay in control. Review what the AI has done, approve or adjust, and send reports directly to clients. You advise — the AI does the groundwork.
